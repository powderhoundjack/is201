Alta: A Powder Skier's Paradise

Alta Ski Area is famous for its massive annual snowfall and commitment to skiing tradition. It remains one of the few resorts in the U.S. that is ski-only (no snowboards allowed), helping to preserve its unique, backcountry-feel experience.

Key Terrain Areas at Alta

Alta offers a wide range of terrain, from high alpine bowls to tight tree runs. Knowing the classic zones is essential for a great powder day:

  1. The Frontside Classics: Accessible from the Collins Lift via High Traverse
    • High Rustler
    • Stone Crusher
    • Westward Ho
  2. The Backside: Accessible from Collins or Sugarloaf Lifts: Requires a hike or long traverse
    • Devil's Castle *pictured above
    • Gunsight
    • East Greely
  3. Supreme: Challenging runs for pow seekers
    • Catherine's Area
    • Supreme Bowl
